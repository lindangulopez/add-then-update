Add, then update: research software metadata in deposits

Where does your metadata get lost on the way to the archive? Bring one software record.

  • Map one record
    The ten fields, one by one: sent to deposit, can be sent, held but not sent, or not held.
  • Slides
    Six slides for the session. Arrow keys or click to move; f for full screen.
  • Demo
    Three anonymised records from one institutional repository: the logic, the results, what they show.
  • Why these ten fields
    How the fields relate to the RSMD guidelines, CodeMeta and the Software Heritage deposit.
  • FAQ
    Why programming language matters if it can be computed, which identifier goes where, how to fix a deposit, and more.
  • Print
    The mapping sheet and the ten-field card (A4, PDF).

The session, about 45 minutes

  1. Round of the table (5 min): who you are, how your repository sends software today.
  2. One picture (5 min): the deposit is the push route into the archive; a SWHID comes back. "Loaded" is not "checked".
  3. Map one record (20 min), on paper or with the survey.
  4. Updating (10 min): a new version, or a metadata-only update.
  5. One next step each (5 min).
